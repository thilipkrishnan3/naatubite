The NaatuBite Story: Authentic Home Kitchens, Pure Traditions

At NaatuBite, we believe authentic South Indian cuisine is not merely food—it is medicine, culture, and nostalgia. Tired of supermarket snacks fried in questionable palm oils and chemical-laden batters with yeast or soda, we decided to bring back grandma’s slow cooking principles.

Every product in our kitchen is prepared using authentic cold-pressed (Marachekku) sesame and groundnut oils, unpolished nutrient-rich native millets, and unrefined country jaggery (Naatu Sakkarai).

🏺

Natural Granite Stone Ground

Our dosa and idly batters are slow-ground in traditional granite wet grinders, keeping the batter cool to nurture natural lactic fermentation for ultra-soft idlies.

🫒

Zero Commercial Palm Oil

We only use cold-pressed groundnut and gingelly oils. No reused oils, no cholesterol-heavy cheap fats, ensuring guilt-free crunch.

🍯

Naatu Sakkarai & Karupatti Only

Our traditional sweets are sweetened with native country sugar and palm jaggery, rich in natural minerals, iron, and slow-release energy.
